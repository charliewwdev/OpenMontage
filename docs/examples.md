# One-line ideas for OpenMontage

Paste one of these ideas into the creation agent, then adjust the script and shots in the workshop. The examples describe a starting point; the final result depends on the models and media providers you configure.

## Short drama (短剧)

| Idea to paste | What to expect |
|:---|:---|
| `Create a three-episode, 9:16 revenge drama: a baker discovers her business partner stole her recipes; open with the bakery's empty shelves.` | A cliffhanger-led outline with a confrontation and a resolution across three short episodes. |
| `Make a three-episode romance about two rival street photographers who keep capturing the same stranger; start with their cameras clicking at once.` | A first-scene hook, recurring character references, and a romantic reveal to develop in the script. |
| `Write a two-episode mystery in one apartment: a tenant receives a voice message from tomorrow; end episode one when the message comes true.` | A compact location and a clear episode break that can guide the storyboard. |

## Viral short video (爆款短视频)

| Idea to paste | What to expect |
|:---|:---|
| `Create a 20-second vertical video showing three ways to rescue an overwatered houseplant; reveal the surprising fix in the first three seconds.` | A quick hook followed by three visual beats and a closing takeaway. |
| `Make a 15-second before-and-after desk makeover for a tiny room, with a reveal at the end and on-screen captions.` | Short, caption-friendly shots built around a visual transformation. |
| `Explain one common coffee-brewing mistake in 25 seconds using close-up shots and a question as the opening hook.` | A question, a demonstration, and a concise payoff suitable for a short feed video. |

## E-commerce product ad (带货)

These ideas follow the built-in product-ad structure: **pain point → product close-ups → benefit captions → call to action**. Replace the sample product and claims with details you can verify.

| Idea to paste | What to expect |
|:---|:---|
| `Make a 20-second vertical ad for a reusable travel mug: show a leaky commute, close-ups of the lid and grip, a caption about easy cleaning, then a shop-now CTA.` | A problem-first sequence with product detail shots and a final action prompt. |
| `Create a 25-second ad for a compact desk lamp: start with an awkward dark workspace, show the adjustable arm and light controls, add clear benefit captions, then a learn-more CTA.` | A visual comparison that moves from the problem to product features and the CTA. |
| `Make a 15-second ad for a foldable grocery tote: open on a bag tearing, show the tote unfolding and being carried, caption its practical benefits, then invite viewers to see the product.` | A fast pain-point hook, close-ups, benefit text, and a closing invitation. |
