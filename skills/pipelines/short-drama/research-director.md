# Research Director — Short Drama

## Process

1. Research genre tropes, platform (Douyin/Reels/Shorts) pacing, vertical framing.
2. Collect 5+ visual/story references (URLs + why they work).
3. Note consistency needs: face lock, wardrobe, location cards.
4. Propose at least 3 differentiated series directions (tone, cast size, episode length).

## Output

Schema-valid `research_brief` with short-drama-specific notes in metadata:
`platform`, `aspect_ratio_recommendation`, `consistency_strategy`.
