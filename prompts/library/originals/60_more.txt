## om-script-001 | 剧本·一句话创意扩成分集大纲 | Script: one-line idea to episode outline | generic | genres/script-method | script | 剧本,大纲,LLM
ZH: 你是竖屏短剧编剧。把下面这句创意扩展成 10 集分集大纲，每集 60–90 秒：每集写出【本集冲突】【爽点或反转】【集末悬念】三行；人物不超过 5 个；第 1 集前 3 秒必须是冲突画面。创意：【在此填写一句话创意】。
EN: You are a vertical short-drama writer. Expand the idea below into a 10-episode outline, 60-90 seconds each: for every episode give three lines—[conflict], [payoff or reversal], [cliffhanger]; no more than five characters; episode 1 must open on a conflict within three seconds. Idea: [your one-line idea].
## om-script-002 | 剧本·单集转分镜脚本 | Script: episode to shot list | generic | genres/script-method | script | 剧本,分镜,LLM
ZH: 把下面这集剧本拆成分镜表，每个镜头一行：镜号｜时长（1.5–3秒）｜景别｜运镜｜画面与动作（一个镜头一个动作，写清身体部位）｜台词｜音效。总时长控制在 60 秒内，保持人物站位和服装连续。剧本：【粘贴剧本】。
EN: Break the episode below into a shot list, one row per shot: # | duration (1.5-3s) | shot size | camera move | image and action (one action per shot, name the body part) | dialogue | SFX. Keep total under 60 seconds and maintain blocking and costume continuity. Script: [paste script].
## om-script-003 | 剧本·台词口语化润色 | Script: make dialogue conversational | generic | genres/script-method | script | 台词,润色,LLM
ZH: 把下面的台词改得更口语、更短：每句不超过 15 个字；删掉解释性的话，用动作代替；保留人物身份差异（霸总简短、闺蜜话多、长辈爱说教）。输出格式：人物（语气）：台词。台词：【粘贴台词】。
EN: Rewrite the dialogue below to be shorter and more conversational: at most about 15 characters (Chinese) or 12 words per line; cut expository lines and replace them with actions; keep voices distinct (the CEO terse, the best friend chatty, elders preachy). Format: Character (tone): line. Dialogue: [paste].
## om-script-004 | 剧本·人物小传生成 | Script: character bio | generic | genres/script-method | character | 人物小传,角色,LLM
ZH: 为下面的角色写一份可用于生成画面的人物小传：外貌（年龄、身高、发型、标志性服装、一个显眼特征）、口头禅、标志性小动作 2 个、核心欲望、最大的秘密。外貌部分用逗号分隔的短语，方便直接复制进生图提示词。角色：【角色名与一句话设定】。
EN: Write a production-ready character bio for the character below: appearance (age, height, hairstyle, signature outfit, one distinctive feature), a catchphrase, two signature gestures, core desire, biggest secret. Write appearance as comma-separated phrases to paste straight into an image prompt. Character: [name and one-line premise].
