## om-act-daily-001 | 日常·清晨醒来 | Waking up in the morning | generic | actions/everyday | storyboard | 日常,清晨,卧室
ZH: 清晨窗帘缝透进一道阳光。闹钟响起，她从被子里伸出一只手摸索着按掉，停顿两秒后猛地坐起，揉着眼睛看向手机，表情瞬间清醒——「迟到了」。中景，暖色晨光。
EN: A shaft of morning sun through a gap in the curtains. The alarm rings; her hand gropes out from under the duvet and switches it off. Two seconds later she bolts upright, rubs her eyes, looks at her phone and is instantly awake—she's late. Medium shot, warm morning light.

## om-act-daily-010 | 日常·走路的五种写法 | Five ways to write walking | generic | actions/everyday | storyboard | 走路,词表,性格
ZH: 「走」要写出状态：①疲惫——拖着脚步、肩膀下垂；②自信——步幅大、抬头挺胸、手臂自然摆动；③心虚——贴着墙走、频频回头；④急切——小跑几步又放慢；⑤失魂——目光空洞、被路人撞到也没反应。
EN: Write the state into the walk: (1) exhausted—dragging feet, shoulders slumped; (2) confident—long strides, chin up, arms swinging; (3) guilty—hugging the wall, glancing back; (4) anxious—breaking into a jog then slowing; (5) dazed—vacant eyes, not reacting when a passer-by bumps into her.

## om-act-emo-003 | 情绪·震惊三段 | Shock in three beats | generic | actions/emotion | storyboard | 震惊,反应,三段式
ZH: 震惊不要只写「很震惊」：①瞳孔放大、动作停住（0.5 秒）；②后退半步、手里的东西滑落；③嘴唇微张却说不出话，呼吸急促。镜头从中景快速推到特写。
EN: Don't just write "shocked": (1) pupils widen, motion freezes (0.5s); (2) half-step back, the object in hand slips and falls; (3) lips part but no words come, breathing quickens. Quick push from medium to close-up.

## om-act-emo-010 | 情绪·眼神戏五种 | Five kinds of eye acting | generic | actions/emotion | storyboard | 眼神,词表,表演
ZH: 眼神比台词有用：①闪躲——目光向下偏移、不敢对视；②挑衅——微抬下巴、眼皮半垂直视；③心疼——眉头微蹙、目光停在对方伤处；④怀疑——眯眼、头微微侧偏；⑤决绝——目光平稳不眨、慢慢移开。
EN: Eyes do more than lines: (1) evasive—gaze drops away, can't hold eye contact; (2) provocative—chin slightly raised, lids half-lowered, direct stare; (3) heartache—brows slightly knit, gaze resting on the other's injury; (4) suspicious—narrowed eyes, head tilted; (5) resolute—steady unblinking gaze, then slowly looks away.

## om-act-emo-014 | 微表情写法要点 | Micro-expression checklist | generic | actions/emotion | storyboard | 要点,特写,表演
ZH: 微表情要配特写或大特写，并只写 1–2 个面部变化：眉（蹙、挑）、眼（眯、睁、眨、泛红）、嘴（抿、颤、上扬一侧）、喉咙（吞咽）、鼻（皱、翕动）。加「表情变化细微自然，不夸张」避免模型做成鬼脸。
EN: Pair micro-expressions with a close-up or extreme close-up and describe only one or two facial changes: brows (knit, raise), eyes (narrow, widen, blink, redden), mouth (press, tremble, one corner rising), throat (swallow), nose (wrinkle, flare). Add "subtle, natural, not exaggerated" so the model doesn't produce a grimace.

## om-act-inter-009 | 多人互动写法要点 | Multi-person interaction checklist | generic | actions/interaction | storyboard | 要点,多人,站位
ZH: 多人镜头先写站位（谁在左、谁在右、谁在前景），再写谁先动、谁反应；每人用不同的服装颜色或外形区分（「穿红裙的女人」「戴眼镜的男人」），避免模型混淆身份。人数尽量不超过 4 人。
EN: In multi-person shots, write the blocking first (who is left, right, foreground), then who moves first and who reacts. Distinguish each person by costume color or look ("the woman in the red dress," "the man with glasses") so the model doesn't swap identities. Keep it to four people or fewer.
