## om-act-formula-001 | 动作提示词万能公式 | Universal action prompt formula | generic | actions/formula | storyboard | 公式,模板,入门
ZH: 【主体+外观】+【动作动词（具体到身体部位）】+【节奏/速度】+【力度与重心变化】+【与环境/道具的物理交互】+【镜头景别与运镜】+【时长分段】。示例：一名穿灰色连帽衫的年轻男子，右脚蹬地、身体前倾，三步加速冲向镜头，第三步时左肩撞开半掩的铁门，铁门反弹发出闷响；中景低机位跟拍，0-2秒加速，2-3秒撞门瞬间轻微慢放。
EN: [Subject + look] + [action verb tied to a body part] + [tempo/speed] + [force and weight shift] + [physical interaction with environment/props] + [shot size and camera move] + [time segments]. Example: A young man in a grey hoodie pushes off with his right foot, leans forward and accelerates in three strides toward the camera; on the third stride his left shoulder bursts through a half-open steel door that rebounds with a dull clang. Medium low-angle tracking shot; 0-2s acceleration, 2-3s slight slow motion on the impact.
## om-act-formula-002 | 一镜一动作原则 | One shot, one action | generic | actions/formula | storyboard | 原则,入门,稳定性
ZH: 每个镜头只写一个主要动作，并写清起点和终点状态。错误：「她走进房间、坐下、打开电脑、哭了起来」。正确：镜头1（0-3秒）：她推门走进昏暗的房间，在门口停住；镜头2（3-6秒）：她坐在桌前，盯着屏幕，下唇轻轻发抖，一滴眼泪滑下。
EN: Give each shot one main action and state its start and end pose. Weak: "She walks in, sits down, opens the laptop and starts crying." Better: Shot 1 (0-3s): she pushes the door open into a dim room and stops at the threshold. Shot 2 (3-6s): she sits at the desk staring at the screen, lower lip trembling, a single tear sliding down.
## om-act-formula-003 | 节奏词速查：快慢与停顿 | Tempo words cheat sheet | generic | actions/formula | storyboard | 节奏,词表,速度
ZH: 快：猛地、骤然、一把、疾步、甩、弹起、连续三下；慢：缓缓、一点点、迟疑地、慢动作、拖着脚步；停顿：动作停在半空、定格一拍、屏住呼吸、手悬在门把上方。写法示例：她猛地转身，抬起的手停在半空一拍，然后缓缓放下。
EN: Fast: suddenly, snaps, whips, darts, springs up, three rapid strikes. Slow: slowly, inch by inch, hesitantly, slow motion, drags her feet. Pause: freezes mid-air, holds for a beat, breath held, hand hovering above the doorknob. Example: She spins around suddenly, her raised hand freezing mid-air for a beat before slowly lowering.
## om-act-formula-004 | 写物理反馈让动作可信 | Add physical feedback | generic | actions/formula | storyboard | 物理,质感,可信度
ZH: 动作后面接「环境的反应」，模型更容易生成可信的运动：脚步→水花溅起/落叶被踢散；挥拳→衣袖甩动、汗珠飞出；摔倒→桌上的杯子震倒、灰尘扬起；关门→门框震动、墙上相框轻晃。示例：他一拳砸在木桌上，桌面的茶杯跳起又落下，茶水溅出一圈。
EN: Follow the action with the environment's reaction so the motion reads as real: footsteps → water splashes or leaves scatter; punch → sleeve whips, sweat flies; fall → a cup on the table tips over, dust rises; door slam → frame shudders, a picture frame rattles. Example: He slams his fist on the wooden table; the teacup jumps and lands, tea splashing in a ring.
## om-act-formula-005 | 身体部位拆解法 | Break the action into body parts | generic | actions/formula | storyboard | 身体,细节,拆解
ZH: 把一个笼统动作拆成「下肢→躯干→上肢→头部→眼神」的顺序。笼统：「他很生气地站起来」。拆解：他双手撑住桌沿，膝盖一挺站起，椅子向后滑开；肩膀耸起，下颌收紧，眼睛死死盯住对面的人。
EN: Split a vague action into legs → torso → arms → head → eyes. Vague: "He stands up angrily." Broken down: He plants both hands on the table edge, straightens his knees and rises, chair skidding back; shoulders lift, jaw tightens, eyes locked on the person opposite.
## om-act-fight-001 | 武打·格挡反击三连 | Martial arts: block, parry, counter | Seedance | actions/fighting | storyboard | 武打,格挡,三段式
ZH: 古装武侠，竹林空地。镜头1（0-2秒）：黑衣刺客持刀从右侧劈下，白衣女侠左臂上抬格挡，刀背擦过护腕迸出火星；镜头2（2-4秒）：她顺势转腰卸力，右掌推向刺客胸口，刺客后退三步，脚下竹叶飞散；镜头3（4-5秒）：她收掌站定，衣摆缓缓落下。中景侧拍，动作清晰，无快速剪切。
EN: Wuxia, bamboo-forest clearing. Shot 1 (0-2s): a black-clad assassin slashes down from the right; the swordswoman in white raises her left forearm to block, the blade's spine scraping her bracer in a spray of sparks. Shot 2 (2-4s): she twists her waist to redirect the force and drives her right palm into his chest; he staggers back three steps, bamboo leaves scattering. Shot 3 (4-5s): she withdraws her palm and settles; her robe hem drifts down. Medium side angle, clear readable choreography, no rapid cuts.
## om-act-fight-002 | 拳击·刺拳加勾拳 | Boxing: jab-hook combo | generic | actions/fighting | storyboard | 拳击,慢动作,汗水
ZH: 地下拳馆，顶光。拳手左脚前踏一步打出两记快速刺拳，紧接着转胯发力打出右勾拳，命中沙袋的瞬间切入 0.5 倍慢动作：沙袋凹陷、汗珠从拳套上甩出、链条摇晃。低机位中近景，轻微手持晃动。
EN: Underground boxing gym, top light. The boxer steps in with the left foot and fires two quick jabs, then rotates the hips into a right hook; at impact on the heavy bag the shot drops to 0.5x slow motion: the bag dents, sweat flicks off the glove, the chain sways. Low-angle medium close-up, slight handheld shake.
## om-act-fight-003 | 街头打斗·推搡升级 | Street scuffle: shove escalates | generic | actions/fighting | storyboard | 冲突,推搡,写实
ZH: 雨夜小巷。穿皮夹克的男人用双手猛推对方胸口，对方踉跄后退撞上垃圾桶，桶盖哐当落地；他稳住身体，抹一把脸上的雨水，攥紧拳头向前一步。写实风格，无血腥，手持跟拍，霓虹反光。

He steadies himself, wipes rain from his face, clenches his fists and steps forward. Realistic, no gore, handheld follow, neon reflections.
## om-act-fight-004 | 剑术·拔剑出鞘特写 | Swordplay: drawing the blade | generic | actions/fighting | storyboard | 拔剑,特写,仪式感

## om-act-fight-006 | 飞踢·腾空侧踢 | Flying side kick | generic | actions/fighting | storyboard | 腾空,踢腿,慢动作

## om-act-fight-007 | 群战·一对多走位 | One against many: blocking choreography | Seedance | actions/fighting | storyboard | 群战,走位,一镜到底
ZH: 一镜到底，镜头环绕主角缓慢旋转。四名打手依次从前、左、后、右扑上：主角侧身躲过第一拳、肘击第二人、低扫第三人小腿、抓住第四人手臂借力摔出。每次攻击间隔约 1 秒，节奏清晰，人物位置不重叠，无血腥。
EN: Single continuous take, camera slowly orbiting the hero. Four thugs attack in order from front, left, back and right: he sidesteps the first punch, elbows the second, sweeps the third's shin, grabs the fourth's arm and throws him using his momentum. About one second between attacks, clear rhythm, no overlapping bodies, no gore.

## om-act-fight-011 | 动作安全写法：受击不写伤口 | Hits without gore | generic | actions/fighting | storyboard | 安全,受击,审核友好
ZH: 受击反应只写身体反馈，不写伤口和血：「被击中后身体向后弓起、踉跄两步、单手捂住肋侧、表情因疼痛扭曲」。这样既有冲击力，也更容易通过平台审核。
EN: Describe hit reactions through body feedback, not wounds or blood: "after the hit his body arches back, he staggers two steps, clutches his ribs with one hand, face twisting in pain." It keeps the impact and passes platform moderation more easily.

## om-act-chase-008 | 追逐镜头写法要点 | Chase shot checklist | generic | actions/chase | storyboard | 要点,运镜,方向
ZH: 写追逐时固定三件事：①方向（从画面左向右跑，全程不反向，避免越轴）；②障碍物（每 2 秒一个：人群、箱子、门、栏杆）；③距离变化（追兵从远到近，或主角拉开距离）。示例：两人始终从左向右奔跑，追兵每过一个障碍就近一步。
EN: Lock three things in a chase: (1) direction—run left to right throughout, never reverse, to avoid crossing the line; (2) obstacles—one every two seconds: crowd, crates, a door, a railing; (3) changing distance—pursuer closing in, or the hero pulling away. Example: both always run left to right; the pursuer gains a step at each obstacle.
