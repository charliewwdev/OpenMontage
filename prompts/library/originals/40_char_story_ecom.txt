## om-char-008 | 角色一致性锁定写法 | Locking character consistency | Seedance | character/consistency | storyboard | 一致性,参考图,锁定
ZH: 使用角色参考图时，在每个镜头提示词开头加「参考图1中的女性」，并重复 2–3 个最显眼的特征（「黑色齐肩短发、米白色西装」），结尾加「人物面部、发型、服装与参考图保持一致」。不要在后续镜头里改写外貌描述。
EN: When using a character reference, start each shot prompt with "the woman from reference image 1," repeat two or three of the most visible traits ("black shoulder-length bob, cream blazer") and end with "face, hair and outfit consistent with the reference." Don't rephrase the appearance in later shots.

## om-char-013 | 角色描述写法要点 | Writing character descriptions | generic | character/guide | character | 要点,角色,外貌
ZH: 角色描述按「年龄+性别+体型→发型发色→五官一个特征→上衣→下装→鞋→配饰」的顺序写，每项一个词组；只写看得见的，不写性格形容词（「善良」画不出来）。
EN: Describe characters in order: age + gender + build → hairstyle and color → one facial feature → top → bottom → shoes → accessories, one phrase each. Write only what's visible; personality adjectives ("kind") can't be drawn.
## om-story-001 | 分镜时序·三镜头 5 秒 | Storyboard timing: three shots in 5s | Seedance | storyboard/timing | storyboard | 时序,多镜头,模板
ZH: 镜头1（0-1.5秒）：【景别】【画面内容】；镜头2（1.5-3.5秒）：【景别】【动作】；镜头3（3.5-5秒）：【景别】【结果/反应】。每个镜头一个动作，镜头之间人物、服装、场景保持一致。
EN: Shot 1 (0-1.5s): [shot size] [content]; Shot 2 (1.5-3.5s): [shot size] [action]; Shot 3 (3.5-5s): [shot size] [result/reaction]. One action per shot; keep characters, costumes and setting consistent across shots.

## om-story-007 | 分镜·时长分配原则 | Storyboard: allocating duration | generic | storyboard/guide | storyboard | 时长,节奏,要点
ZH: 竖屏短剧单镜头一般 1.5–3 秒；动作镜头 1–2 秒，情绪特写 2–3 秒，建立镜头不超过 2 秒。生成模型单次 5–10 秒时，拆成 2–4 个镜头比一个长镜头更稳定。
EN: In vertical shorts a single shot usually runs 1.5-3s: action shots 1-2s, emotional close-ups 2-3s, establishing shots no more than 2s. When the model generates 5-10s per run, splitting into two to four shots is steadier than one long take.
## om-story-008 | 分镜·连续性检查 | Storyboard: continuity check | generic | storyboard/guide | storyboard | 连续性,穿帮,检查
ZH: 每个镜头生成前检查：①人物左右位置是否和上一镜一致（不越轴）；②手里的道具是否还在同一只手；③服装、发型、天气、光线方向是否一致。在提示词里显式写出这些状态。
EN: Before each shot, check: (1) are characters on the same sides as the last shot (don't cross the line)? (2) is the prop still in the same hand? (3) are costume, hair, weather and light direction consistent? State these explicitly in the prompt.
## om-story-009 | 分镜·首尾帧控制 | Storyboard: first and last frame control | generic | storyboard/keyframe | storyboard | 首尾帧,图生视频,控制
ZH: 用首帧图（女主站在门口）+ 尾帧图（女主坐在沙发上），提示词只写中间过程：「她慢慢走进客厅，把包放在茶几上，疲惫地坐下」。首尾帧决定构图，提示词负责动作。
EN: Use a first-frame image (heroine at the door) and last-frame image (heroine on the sofa); the prompt only describes the transition: "She walks slowly into the living room, sets her bag on the coffee table and sits down wearily." Keyframes fix the composition; the prompt drives the action.
## om-story-010 | 分镜·六格情绪弧线 | Storyboard: six-panel emotional arc | generic | storyboard/panel-template | storyboard | 六格,情绪弧线,模板
ZH: 1 平静（全景日常）→ 2 打破（近景收到消息）→ 3 否认（中景摇头）→ 4 崩溃（特写落泪）→ 5 决定（近景擦泪抬头）→ 6 行动（全景起身出门）。同一角色、同一服装。

## om-ecom-010 | 电商广告写法要点 | E-commerce prompt checklist | generic | ecommerce/guide | storyboard | 要点,电商,合规
ZH: ①用 [产品]/[品牌] 占位，生成后替换；②写「产品形状、标签、颜色全程一致，不变形」；③效果对比不要夸大（避免「瞬间」「100%」）；④不出现真实名人和其他品牌；⑤前 2 秒就让产品出现。
EN: (1) Use [product]/[brand] placeholders and swap after generating; (2) add "product shape, label and color stay consistent, no deformation"; (3) don't exaggerate before/after claims (avoid "instantly," "100%"); (4) no real celebrities or other brands; (5) show the product within the first two seconds.
