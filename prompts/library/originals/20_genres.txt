## om-genre-template-001 | 题材通用模板：人物+困境+反击 | Genre template: character + predicament + strike back | generic | genres/template | script | 模板,剧本,结构
ZH: 「【人物身份】在【场景】遭遇【具体羞辱/困境】，【旁观者】的反应是【反应】；就在【时间点】，【转折事件】出现，【人物】做出【反击动作】，【对手】的表情从【A】变为【B】。」把每个括号换成可拍的画面，而不是形容词。
EN: "[Character] faces [specific humiliation/predicament] at [location]; [bystanders] react with [reaction]. At [moment], [turning event] happens; [character] [strikes back with an action], and [opponent]'s expression shifts from [A] to [B]." Fill every bracket with something filmable, not an adjective.
## om-genre-template-002 | 一集短剧结构（60 秒） | One-episode structure (60s) | generic | genres/template | script | 结构,单集,节奏
ZH: 0-3秒：冲突画面直接开场；3-15秒：交代人物关系和困境；15-40秒：矛盾升级，对手步步紧逼；40-55秒：反转或反击；55-60秒：新的悬念（一个电话、一个人出现、一句话）。每段对应 1–3 个镜头。
EN: 0-3s: open straight on the conflict; 3-15s: set up relationships and the predicament; 15-40s: escalate as the opponent presses; 40-55s: reversal or counterattack; 55-60s: a new cliffhanger (a call, an arrival, one line). One to three shots per segment.
